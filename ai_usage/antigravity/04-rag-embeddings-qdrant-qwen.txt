PROMPT / INTERACTION 14: Calibrated Reasoning Quality, Forensic Standards & Evidence Hierarchy
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[USER REQUEST]:
Improve the reasoning quality of the TraceMind RAG/agent system without rebuilding the app:
1. Physical Object vs Digital Identifier: Tag/beacon/log movement != physical object movement. If maintenance logs show physical cart was immobilized at CB-2 with battery removed, conclude: "The tag moved, but the physical cart did not."
2. Person vs Credential: Credential packet logged != physical person present. (Packet replayed through R-19).
3. Association vs Responsibility: Access, proximity, or tool installation indicates association, NOT direct operation or guilt ("strongly associated with", "not directly proven").
4. Fact vs Unsupported Inference: Do not invent unstated motives (e.g. TP-6 caused C-7 outage; do not invent theft motive).
5. Timeline Check: Cause must precede effect (cause time <= effect time; 22:11 event cannot cause 22:07 event).
6. Conflict Resolution Hierarchy: Tested forensic evidence & physical records > provisional witness guesses; corrected statements > retracted initial statements; later forensic testing > initial logging ("initially recorded as X, but later forensic testing established Y").
7. Insufficient Evidence / Abstention: Clearly state "The available evidence does not establish this."
8. Calibrated Tone & Human-Readable Citations: [FileName, Page X], "No conflicting evidence was found among the reviewed sources."
